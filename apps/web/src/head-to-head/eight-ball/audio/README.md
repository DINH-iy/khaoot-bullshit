# Pool impact recordings

Both clips were recorded by Freesound user mccarthy@bedmas.com and are licensed under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- [Ball strike](https://freesound.org/s/42365/) was trimmed to the 56 ms impact at 0.337 s.
- [Cushion rebound](https://freesound.org/s/42364/) was trimmed to the 76 ms impact at 0.376 s.

The clips are peak-normalized mono PCM with short fades. The measured spectral centroid of the attack was approximately 2.3 kHz for the ball strike and 430 Hz for the cushion rebound.
